Privacy Policy for PickLog

Last updated: 6 February 2026

Overview

PickLog is a locksport practice tracking app designed for lock picking enthusiasts. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

PickLog does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data you enter into the app (sessions, locks, belt progress) is stored locally on your device only.

Data Storage

All app data is stored on your device using local storage. No data is sent to external servers. No analytics or tracking services are used.

Third-Party Services

PickLog does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising frameworks.

Children's Privacy

PickLog does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
